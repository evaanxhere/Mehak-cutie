kuch toh tha

jo ab nahi hai. aur theek hai woh bhi.

Yaad hai jab
sab normal tha?

Random texts. Stupid jokes. Tujhe kuch chahiye hota tha, tu maangti thi. Main no bolta tha, tu irritate hoti thi. Phir bhi baat hoti thi. Woh jo cycle thi — honestly, miss karta hoon usse. Even the annoying parts.

Main yeh nahi keh raha
ki sab waisa hi ho jaaye —

Woh toh shayad possible nahi. Aur main itna bhi filmy nahi hoon ki "sab theek ho jaayega" wala scene karun. Bas yeh hai ki — jo bhi tha woh achha tha. Tu achhi thi. Woh time achha tha. Usse erase karna nahi chahta memory se.

Tu abhi bhi matter karti hai,
chahe baat ho ya na ho.

Yeh koi drama nahi hai. Koi "please wapas aa" wali baat bhi nahi. Bas sach hai — kuch log hote hain jo chale bhi jaate hain aur phir bhi ek specific jagah occupy karte hain. Tere liye woh jagah empty nahi hai.

Aur agar kabhi
baat karni ho —

Toh kar lena. Seriously. Main awkward nahi karunga, promise. Aur agar nahi karni toh woh bhi theek hai. Life chalne ki cheez hai, aur hum dono apni apni direction mein chal rahe hain. But know karna chahta tha ki — door sahi, good wishes tere liye hain. Always.

bas itna hi —

Take care of yourself,
Mehak.
genuinely.
